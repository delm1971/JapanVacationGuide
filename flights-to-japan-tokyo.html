Narita (NRT) vs. Haneda (HND): Which Tokyo Airport is Best?

Tokyo is uniquely served by two massive international airports. While many travelers assume they are interchangeable, your airport choice significantly impacts both your flight cost and your post-arrival transit experience:

  • Tokyo Haneda Airport (HND): Located just 9 miles south of central Tokyo. Haneda is incredibly convenient, allowing you to reach major neighborhoods like Shibuya or Shinjuku in about 30 minutes via the Tokyo Monorail or Keikyu Line. Because of its premium location, flights here can occasionally carry a modest price premium.
  • Tokyo Narita Airport (NRT): Located roughly 37 miles east of Tokyo in Chiba Prefecture. While the train journey into the city takes roughly an hour aboard the Narita Express (NEX) or Skyliner, Narita handles a massive volume of international traffic and serves as the primary base for low-cost carriers. This means Narita is frequently home to the cheapest base airfares.

Typical Price Ranges by Season

To budget accurately for your journey to Tokyo, you must account for the dramatic pricing swings driven by tourism seasonality:

Low Season (January to February)

The absolute best time for bargain hunters. Post-holiday winter travel drops off significantly, meaning you can easily find round-trip economy flights ranging from $600 to $900. Tokyo is crisp and less crowded during these months, making it a fantastic time to explore.

Shoulder Season (May, September to November)

The perfect sweet spot balancing beautiful weather and moderate pricing. Late spring and autumn (vivid fall foliage season) offer incredibly comfortable walking weather, with typical round-trip fares holding steady between $900 and $1,300.

Peak Season (Late March to April, June to August)

Expect maximum crowds and premium ticket costs. The world-famous Cherry Blossom season and the summer school holiday break cause surge pricing, driving standard economy tickets up to $1,400 to $2,000+. Booking at least 6–8 months in advance is critical if you intend to travel during these periods.

Airline Quality Pro-Tip: If you are choosing between similar prices on economy seats, Japanese flag carriers **Japan Airlines (JAL)** and **All Nippon Airways (ANA)** regularly win global aviation awards for offering superior legroom configurations (frequently a 2-4-2 layout rather than the cramped 3-4-3 layout found on many long-haul Western fleets), along with stellar onboard dining and hospitality.
